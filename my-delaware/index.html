Your Delaware, by address

Type a Delaware street address to see your State Senator and Representative, your county council or levy court member, your school district report card and every race on your 2026 ballot, from public records.

How to read this. One address, the public record that goes with it. Districts come from the U.S. Census Bureau's geocoder; officeholders from the official pages listed in the Delaware Office Registry (checked 2026-10-14); school figures from the Department of Education. We rank no one and recommend nothing. Confirm your districts and polling place at ivote.de.gov.

Look up an address

Your address is sent to the U.S. Census Bureau's public geocoder to find your districts; the Census Bureau keeps its own server records. First State Lens does not store or log it. A ZIP code alone shows the districts it overlaps.

What the lookup shows

  • Your State Senator and State Representative, and their recorded votes.
  • Your county council or levy court district and who holds it (New Castle, Kent, Sussex).
  • Your public school district and its report card.
  • Every race on your 2026 ballot.
  • Drinking water: we cannot match an address to a water system yet, because no public service-area map covers every system. Find yours by name or town on Is my water safe?
  • Child care: see licensed child care by county.
