Every grant, contract, loan, and direct payment the federal government spent in Delaware in a fiscal year — by agency, recipient, and type. Pulled directly from the public USAspending.gov record.

How to read this. These are facts from the federal government's own public spending record (USAspending.gov), shown without interpretation. Figures count money by place of performance in Delaware — work done in the state — which is different from money paid to Delaware-based organizations. Large grant figures often reflect federal-to-state pass-through programs (for example Medicaid), not discretionary awards. First State Lens adds no commentary on whether any spending is good or bad.
