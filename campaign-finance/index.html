What Delaware's federal candidates raise and spend

Money raised and spent by candidates for U.S. House and U.S. Senate from Delaware in the current election cycle, as reported to the Federal Election Commission. Pulled directly from the FEC's public record.

How to read this. These are the candidates' own filings with the Federal Election Commission, shown without interpretation. Amounts are as-filed: the most recent report can lag current fundraising, and a candidate who filed no report shows no receipts. Party is the FEC's own factual label, not a signal — First State Lens does not rank candidates, colour them by party, or suggest who is ahead. Appearing here means a candidate registered a committee with the FEC for this cycle; that is not the same as being certified on the Delaware ballot. First State Lens adds no commentary.
