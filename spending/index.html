How to read this. This section uses a different source from the Online Checkbook figures above: the state's Statewide Central Contract Spend dataset, published by the Office of Management and Budget, Government Support Services (GSS). It reports, month by month, the spend identified with each vendor on the statewide central contracts GSS runs for state agencies, school districts and municipalities. It does not list every contract the state signs, so its totals are not comparable with the Checkbook totals above.

The dataset has no contract start or end dates and no contract award values. "First month" and "Last month" below are the first and last months with spend reported in this dataset, which begins in July 2022. They are not contract start or end dates. A negative amount is shown as the state reports it.

Award type, in the state's words: GSS means contracts awarded by Government Support Services through solicitation or cooperative agreement; GSA means contracts GSS negotiated with vendors on U.S. General Services Administration schedules. Vendor names appear exactly as the state publishes them, with spacing tidied; we add no addresses or other details. The table is sorted by contract description, A to Z, and is not a ranking.
