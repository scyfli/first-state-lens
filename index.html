The Delaware Grocery Initiative has deployed ~$2.15M across five award cycles to fight food deserts. 125,370 Delawareans remain food insecure. About this site
First State Lens is a Delaware civic outcomes platform. Each dashboard tracks a specific legislative initiative against measurable real-world results, with every published number sealed to a methodology version and a public source. The analytical CMS — methodology source-of-truth, source registry, lineage graph, briefs — is maintained in a separate private workspace; published methodology is mirrored to a public Quartz site (methodology.firststatelens.com, forthcoming). Built by FirmSide AI · Civic Analytics Lab. The brand is deliberately decoupled from any single stakeholder.
